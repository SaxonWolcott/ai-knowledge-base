# 3Blue1Brown (Grant Sanderson)

A YouTube channel by **Grant Sanderson** known for animation-driven mathematical explainers, including a widely-cited *Deep Learning* series that builds [[neural-network]] intuition visually from first principles.

## Why it matters to this wiki

3Blue1Brown is a high-quality **educational** source: rigorous, visual, and aimed at conceptual intuition rather than news or benchmarks. Per §7, treat its videos as `youtube-lecture` — credibility high for foundational/established material, with the usual care that pedagogical *motivations* (e.g. the edge-detector "hope" in [[neural-network]]) are flagged as such rather than recorded as established fact.

The animations are produced with **Manim**, Sanderson's open-source Python animation library — which is *why* these videos are visual-heavy and a good test case for the `/ingest-youtube` visual-capture path.
